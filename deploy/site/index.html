JobScout Discover

Multi-source job discovery for AI assistants. Searches the job sources this service has enabled, merges duplicate listings and shows where each one was found.

What it does not do

  • It does not rank jobs for you.
  • It does not store a CV, contact employers or apply on your behalf.
  • It keeps no profile or search history between requests.

Connect

MCP endpoint (Streamable HTTP): {{placeholder "http.request.scheme"}}://{{.Req.Host}}/mcp

Use it from ChatGPT or Codex through the JobScout Discover plugin, or add it as a custom connector in an MCP client. Install notes are in the project README.
